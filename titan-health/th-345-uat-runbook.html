Pipeline Overview

An Encounter is eligible for archival when it has been in a closed status (Closed or Confirmed No Opportunity) for at least 730 days. Each weekend, Salesforce flags eligible Encounters, Tim's integration pulls them into Athena, and Salesforce hard-deletes the originals after a 7-day buffer.

 Sun 02:00  EncounterArchivalBatch        →  Archival_Status__c = 'Archiving In Progress'
 (async)    Tim's integration             →  REST /services/apexrest/archival/encounters
                                            Writes to Athena, stamps 'Archive Completed' +
                                            Archive_Completed_Date__c
 Sun 04:00  EncounterDeleteBatch          →  Hard-deletes where Archive Completed ≥ 7 days
 (every)    Archive_Run__c row            →  Audit log for each batch execution
